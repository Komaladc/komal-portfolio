Featured Projects

Cyber Security Symposium 2025

Program Coordinator | Cybersecurity · National-Level Conference

A national-level cybersecurity initiative supported by the Ministry of Communication and Information Technology (MoCIT), bringing together cybersecurity professionals, government representatives, academics, industry experts, and students to strengthen Nepal's cybersecurity ecosystem. The symposium focused on cybersecurity awareness, emerging cyber threats, digital safety, information security, and multi-stakeholder collaboration through expert discussions, technical sessions, and knowledge-sharing activities.

NEC Ingenium 2025

Program Coordinator | Technology · Innovation · Student Engagement

A college-level technical and innovation event organized by Nepal Engineering College, providing a platform for students to showcase engineering projects, demonstrate technical capabilities, and explore emerging technologies. Contributed to program coordination, event planning, and stakeholder engagement while supporting creativity, interdisciplinary collaboration, practical learning, and innovative problem-solving.

Cyber Security Symposium 2026

Program Coordinator & Chairperson | Cybersecurity · Technology Policy · Digital Transformation

A national-level cybersecurity initiative organized under the theme Building a Secure and Resilient Digital Nepal, bringing together policymakers, government institutions, cybersecurity professionals, researchers, academics, and industry representatives. The four-day initiative featured expert discussions, research presentations, cybersecurity awareness sessions, and policy-oriented dialogues, emphasizing institutional collaboration, national cybersecurity preparedness, and digital resilience.

TechSprint Hackathon 2026 – Code for Digital Nepal

Program Coordinator | Hackathon · Innovation · Cybersecurity

A national-level hackathon co-organized with the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology (MoEST), encouraging students, developers, researchers, and technology enthusiasts to develop innovative solutions for real-world challenges in Nepal's digital ecosystem. The competition received over 160 registrations, with 25 selected teams advancing to the hackathon. It also featured the NCHL Pen Testing Challenge, giving participants opportunities to demonstrate technical expertise, practical cybersecurity skills, creative thinking, and solution-oriented development.

Municipal AI Governance Conference 2026

Technical Expert – Representing CSRI Nepal | Artificial Intelligence · Public Policy · Digital Governance

A municipal-level conference organized under the theme Reshaping Municipal Governance in the Age of Artificial Intelligence, bringing together policymakers, technical experts, researchers, and local government stakeholders to explore AI in public administration and municipal services. Representing the Centre for Cyber Security Research and Innovation Nepal (CSRI Nepal) as a Technical Expert, contributed perspectives on responsible AI adoption, cybersecurity, data privacy, digital public infrastructure, and institutional readiness, supporting discussions on the Municipal AI Declaration.

Asia Pacific Regional Internet Governance Forum (APrIGF) 2026

Drafting Committee Member & Organizing Committee Member | Internet Governance · Digital Policy · Regional Cooperation

An Asia-Pacific regional multi-stakeholder Internet governance initiative bringing together government representatives, civil society, academia, the technical community, private-sector stakeholders, and young professionals to discuss challenges and opportunities shaping the digital landscape. Contributed to drafting and organizational responsibilities and collaborative discussions on cybersecurity, artificial intelligence governance, digital rights, data privacy, Internet accessibility, digital inclusion, and regional cooperation.

EDTREX Learning Platform (Learning Management System)

A digital learning management system designed to facilitate online education, academic resource management, and interactive learning experiences. The platform aims to provide a centralized environment for students, educators, and administrators to manage courses, access learning materials, monitor academic progress, and streamline educational activities through technology-driven solutions.

Consultancy Management System

A centralized consultancy management system developed to streamline consultancy operations, student information management, admission processing, application tracking, and administrative workflows. The system aims to improve operational efficiency by digitizing consultancy processes, organizing student records, managing admission enquiries, and providing a structured platform for communication and coordination.

AI-Based Accident Detection System

An artificial intelligence-powered accident detection system designed to identify road accidents through intelligent monitoring and automated detection technologies. The project focuses on leveraging AI-based analysis to recognize potential accident situations, facilitate faster incident identification, and support timely emergency response, contributing to improved road safety and intelligent transportation systems.

FoodScan

A technology-driven food information and scanning platform designed to help users access relevant food-related information through digital solutions. The project focuses on simplifying food information discovery, improving accessibility, and exploring technology-based approaches to support informed food choices and digital food management.

Cyber Fraud Detection System

A cybersecurity-focused system designed to identify and analyze potential fraudulent digital activities using intelligent detection techniques. The project explores approaches to recognizing suspicious patterns, analyzing potentially fraudulent transactions, and strengthening digital security. It aims to contribute to safer digital transactions, fraud awareness, and improved protection against emerging cyber threats.
